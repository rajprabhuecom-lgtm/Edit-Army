**Client:** Coach Shanmugam, Herbalife Nutrition (independent associate), Namakkal · **Prepared by:** Prabhuraj, ProfitSoul
**Goal:** book appointments to explain the wellness business opportunity. **CTA:** "Keela irukkura link-ah click panni, unga appointment-ai ippove book pannunga."

## Script (as supplied, Tanglish)

1. Vanakkam Namakkal makkalaye!
2. "Health-ah care pannanum-nu ninaikkureenga… aana doctor consultation, tests, expenses-nu yosichu regular check-up kooda postpone panreengala?"
3. Ippo namma lifestyle maarittu irukku. Adhanala nutrition, fitness, healthy habits & wellness guidance-ku demand-um increase aagittu irukku.
4. Indha growing wellness industry-la, unga health-ai care pannikitte, oru business opportunity-um explore panna mudiyum.
5. He is coach Shanmugam, Herbalife Nutrition-oda work pannitu irukken. Inga nutrition, healthy habits, fitness & business skills pathi training, mentor support, team guidance and structured system moolama learn panna opportunity irukku.
6. Working professionals, homemakers, fitness enthusiasts… wellness industry-la pudhusa learn panni business opportunity explore panna interested-aa?
7. Neenga thaniya figure out panna thevai illa. Proper training-um guidance-um support-um irukkum.
8. Keela irukkura link-ah click panni, unga appointment-ai ippove book pannunga.
9. Appointment-la complete details-ah explain panrom.
10. Unga health-um personal growth-um orae journey-la start pannunga!

## Compliance decisions

- **No before/after weight photos in this ad.** `docs/b-roll-framework.md` §7 bans weight-loss transformations in wellness ads, the script never mentions weight loss, and putting weight results next to a business opportunity mixes a health claim with a recruitment pitch. Keep them for a separate organic testimonial Reel, with written consent.
- **No income visuals.** The script makes no earnings claim, so B-roll shows no money, cars, lifestyle or ₹ figures.
- **Herbalife name/logo:** use the name as spoken; use the logo only if the coach has an approved asset under Herbalife's distributor rules.
