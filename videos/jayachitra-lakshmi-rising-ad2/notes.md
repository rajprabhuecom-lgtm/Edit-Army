### What I found
1. **Same resolution problem as Ad 1:** 480×854, WhatsApp-compressed. We need the phone originals.
2. **The setting doesn't match the brief.** The script asks for a bright saree, a podcast mic and a maroon and gold background. She is seated at a table in the same green kurta, with the pooja shelf behind her and an **open notebook (her script) visible at the bottom of the frame**. The lower third and the bottom captions cover most of the notebook. Her eyes stay on camera, so it works.
3. **One script line was not recorded:** "Mariyaadhaiyum selvamum ungala thedi varum" ("respect and wealth will come to you", 37–40 s in the script). Clip 7 ends at "…life-la kondu vaanga". The edit flows without it. If she wants it in, she needs to record one 3 s clip.
4. **Clip 7 has extra words:** "Promotion, salary hike, sontha car, sontha veedu, ivai ellaam manifestation moolama, unga career moolama, unga life-la kondu vaanga". That's fine, and the captions follow what she said.
5. **Audio** sits around −27 dB mean. The same voice chain as Ad 1 (denoise, compressor, −14 LUFS) will handle it.
