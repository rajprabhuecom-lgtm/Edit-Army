# Coach Deepa ad: Google Flow prompts

Generate **2 takes** of each shot and keep the better one. Upload to the Drive folder **Deepa - B-roll Ad (faceless) / B-roll uploads**, named by ID (for example `D02_working_woman.mp4`).

Every shot is vertical 9:16, 6–8 s, with slow camera moves (the edit speeds up). No text, numbers or logos in any shot. Words and numbers are added as graphics in the edit.

Flow is only for **scene-setting** shots. Coach Deepa, the centre, members and results always come from the real photos and videos.
