## Flags

1. **Consent.** Every member in the result photos needs written consent to appear in a paid ad.
2. **Meta policy.** Meta usually rejects weight-loss ads with side-by-side before/after images (09, 14, 12) and copy that addresses a personal health attribute ("Neenga overweight-ah? PCOD?"). Safer ad version: show members' "after" photos and centre life, not split before/after, and keep the problem line general. Use the full version for organic Reels.
3. **Herbalife branding** is visible in the centre footage. If Coach Deepa is a Herbalife distributor, check their advertising rules on weight-loss claims.
