**Mood arc, following the final timeline:**

| Final time | Beat | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6.6 s | Pain: "forgot to live for you" | Soft veena or bansuri phrase over a low string pad. Sparse and a little wistful. |
| 6.6–13 s | Dream and the turn to "manifestation" | A gentle lift: light mridangam or tabla pattern and a rising string swell, plus a sparkle SFX on the word "Manifestation". |
| 13–32.5 s | Method and dream montage | Full uplifting groove at about 90–100 BPM: veena or flute melody, strings and soft percussion. A whoosh on each B-roll cut and a chime on each tick. |
| 32.5–35.5 s | "Naanum oru pennaa…" | Drop to soft piano or tanpura only, about −6 dB, so her story lands. |

| 49.5–58 s | CTA | Peak and resolve. A bell or temple-bell accent on "Lakshmi Rising", then a clean ending on the end card. |

**Where to get it (best option first):**
1. **An AI-generated original track** (Suno or Udio on a *paid* plan, which includes a commercial licence). Prompt: `Uplifting Tamil Carnatic-fusion instrumental for a women's empowerment ad, veena and bansuri flute lead, warm strings, light mridangam and tabla, soft piano breakdown in the middle, 95 BPM, hopeful and confident, builds to a bright finish, no vocals, 60 seconds.` Generate 4 versions and pick the one that matches the arc.
2. **Licensed stock libraries:** Artlist, Epidemic Sound or Envato Elements. Search for "Indian inspirational", "Carnatic fusion", "veena uplifting", "Indian corporate motivational" or "South Indian cinematic hope", and download the 60 s edit.
3. **Free for Meta ads:** Meta Sound Collection (in Creator Studio and Business Suite). Search "Indian" or "Inspirational". This is safe for Facebook and Instagram ads only.
4. **No licence at all:** `engine/base/audio/synth.py` can generate an original procedural bed. It is less "Tamil" in character but carries zero copyright risk.

**Never** use Tamil film songs, BGMs or their instrumental, cover or karaoke versions (Ilaiyaraaja, A.R. Rahman, Anirudh, Harris Jayaraj and so on). The composition itself is copyrighted, and Meta will mute or reject the ad. They can serve as mood references only.

Mix the voice at −14 LUFS with the music ducked to about −22 dB under speech.
