# City ad: Google Flow prompts

Generate **2 takes** of each shot and keep the better one. Name each file by its ID (for example `B05_gst_traffic.mp4`) and put it in the Drive folder `For Client's editing / Coach Muthu / city-broll/`.

Every shot is vertical 9:16, 8 s long, with slow camera moves (the edit speeds up 1.2×). No text appears in any shot. Place names, times and numbers are added as graphics in the edit.

**Note on real places:** Flow can't show the real Nandeeswarar temple or the real GST Road. The prompts ask for a *typical* South Indian scene of each, and the place name comes from the coach's voice plus a caption. That keeps the ad honest: we never claim the clip is the real spot.
