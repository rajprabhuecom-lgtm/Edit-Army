# Fish ad: Google Flow prompts

Generate **2 takes** of each shot and keep the better one. Name each file by its ID (for example `B01_fish_food.mp4`) and put it in the Drive folder `For Client's editing / Coach Muthu / fish-broll/`.

Every shot is vertical 9:16, 8 s long, with slow camera moves (the edit speeds up 1.2×). No text appears in any shot. Numbers and labels are added as graphics in the edit.

**Keep the tank the same in every fish shot:** a rectangular glass home aquarium about 2 ft wide on a wooden stand, green plants, small orange and silver fish. That way B01, B02, B03 and B11 look like one tank.
